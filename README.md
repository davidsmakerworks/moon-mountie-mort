# Moon Mountie Mort

The year is 2112, and Canada has terraformed the moon into a winter wonderland! American criminal
Buford T. Bilgewater has snuck past Moon Canadian customs dressed as a penguin, and he's out to smash
every delicate snow globe needed for Moon Canada Day.

Help Mort of the Royal Canadian Moon Police catch the snow globes and drop them in the collection box
until Moon Mechanic Milo fixes the teleporter and whisks them off to safety. Mort can only carry two
globes at a time, so when his hands are full he has to jump and bump globes back into the sky.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's no build step and nothing to install. The
retro font loads from Google Fonts, and the game falls back to a monospace font when offline.

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys or A / D | Slide the pointer or your finger |
| Jump (bump globes back up) | Space, Up, or W | Click or tap |
| Moon shoes (2× speed for 10 seconds) | S or Down | Tap the skate button under the maple leaves |
| Pause | P or Esc | |
| Mute | M | |

## Scoring

- **$5 CAD ($3 USD)** for each globe Mort catches (on the ground, anywhere above his knees) or bumps off his hat (while jumping)
- **$10 CAD ($6 USD)** for each globe delivered to the collection box beside the teleporter
- **$25 CAD ($15 USD)** for stunning Buford with a bumped globe (it also stops his throwing for a moment)
- **$10 CAD** for every globe still in the air, or in Mort's hands, when the teleporter beams them away
- **Level bonus** of $50 × the level number
- Every $500 CAD earns an extra maple leaf (life) and an extra pair of moon shoes

Each broken globe costs one maple leaf. When Mort's hands are full, globes fall right past him, so jump! Every level, Buford keeps more globes in the air, throws them
faster, and Milo needs a bit longer to finish the repairs. The high score is saved in your browser.

If you leave the title screen alone, an attract-mode demo starts playing, just like in the arcade.

## Moon shoes

Mort starts each game with 3 pairs of moon shoes. Putting them on makes him move twice as fast for
10 seconds. They look exactly like hockey skates, but they are definitely moon shoes.
