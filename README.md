# Moon Mountie Mort

The year is 2112, and Canada has terraformed the moon into a winter wonderland! American criminal
Buford T. Bilgewater has snuck past Moon Canadian customs dressed as a penguin, and he's out to smash
every delicate snow globe needed for Moon Canada Day.

Help Mort of the Royal Canadian Moon Police juggle the snow globes back into the sky until Moon Mechanic Milo
fixes the teleporter and whisks them off to safety.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's no build step and nothing to install. The
retro font loads from Google Fonts, and the game falls back to a monospace font when offline.

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys or A / D | Slide the pointer or your finger |
| Jump (bump globes higher) | Space, Up, or W | Click or tap |
| Pause | P or Esc | |
| Mute | M | |

## Scoring

- **$5 CAD ($3 USD)** each time a globe bounces off Mort's hat
- **$25 CAD ($15 USD)** for stunning Buford with a bumped globe (it also stops his throwing for a moment)
- **$10 CAD** for every globe still in the air when the teleporter beams them away
- **Level bonus** of $50 × the level number
- An extra maple leaf (life) every $1,000 CAD

Each broken globe costs one maple leaf. Every level, Buford keeps more globes in the air, throws them
faster, and Milo needs a bit longer to finish the repairs. The high score is saved in your browser.

If you leave the title screen alone, an attract-mode demo starts playing, just like in the arcade.
